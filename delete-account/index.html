Delete your Boxstead account

Cortexlab Studios · Boxstead (com.cortexlabstudios.boxstead)

You can ask us to delete your Boxstead account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion
  1. Email fahimgul338@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up.
  2. Use the subject Boxstead account deletion and include your username if you remember it.
  3. We'll confirm and delete your account, normally within 30 days.

Email deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and sign-in credentials
  • Your username and profile
  • Online match and queue records linked to your account

What isn't deleted

  • Data stored only on your device (settings, stats, achievements). Uninstall the game or clear its storage to remove it.
  • Anonymous or unlinked match records that can no longer be tied to you.
  • Data held by advertising networks (Unity, Meta). Reset your advertising ID in Android settings and see their policies.
No account? If you only played offline, we hold no account data about you and there's nothing to delete on our side.

For details on how we handle data, read our privacy policy.
