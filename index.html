Privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026. Boxstead is a strategy board game made by Cortexlab Studios.

The short version

  • You can play every mode, including online matches, without creating an account.
  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, and the ad networks it works with collect device identifiers such as your Advertising ID.
  • If you do make an account, you can ask us to delete it at any time.

What stays on your device

Some information is saved on your phone and is not uploaded to us:

  • Which achievements you've unlocked
  • How many matches you've played, which unlocks board themes
  • Your chosen board theme
  • Whether you've already been asked to rate the game

If you sign in to an account, a login session is also stored on your device so you stay signed in. Uninstalling Boxstead or clearing its app data removes all of this.

Online matches

Online play needs a place for two phones to share the state of a match. When you play Quick Match or use a room code, we store:

  • The display name, color, and symbol you chose
  • The room code, the board, the score, and whose turn it is
  • For Quick Match, a temporary matchmaking entry (your name, color, symbol, and board size) until you're paired with an opponent or cancel

This is used only to run the match. We don't link it to your device or to an account. Your opponent can see your display name, so avoid putting personal information in it.

Accounts

Creating an account is optional. If you create one, we collect your email address, a username, and a password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our account provider, so we can't read them.

We use this information to sign you in and show your username. We don't use your email for marketing.

Ads

Boxstead shows full-screen ads, and may show other ad formats later. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, a mediation service that requests ads from several ad networks, including Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network. The networks we use may change over time.

To choose, show, and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these networks may collect:

  • Your Android Advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which gives an approximate location
  • Device and operating system details, and the app version
  • How you interact with ads

Each network handles this data under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads)
  • Meta (Audience Network)

You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your phone's Android settings. The exact location varies by device, but it's usually under Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads.

Other services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database. It stores account information and online match data. Its servers may be in a different country from yours. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • Google Play delivers the app and shows the in-app rating prompt. If you leave a review, Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy, and it appears on the Play Store listing like any other review.
  • Update check. When the app opens, it asks our server for the latest published version number so it can tell you if an update is available. This request carries no personal information beyond what any internet request includes, such as your IP address.

Sharing your result

After a match you can share a result card using your phone's share sheet. The image is created on your device, and nothing is shared unless you tap Share and choose where it goes.

How long we keep data

  • Online match and matchmaking records are temporary. We delete old ones on a regular basis.
  • Account information is kept until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Data held by ad networks follows their own retention policies.

Your choices and rights

You can play without an account, reset your Advertising ID, and uninstall the app at any time.

You can ask us to give you a copy of the account information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To delete your account, follow the steps on the account deletion page. Depending on where you live, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional legal rights over your personal data. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data between the app and our servers is sent over encrypted connections, and passwords are stored hashed. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Boxstead is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll say so in the app's update notes.
