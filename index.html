How to play

Easy to learn in a minute. Hard to master.

  1. Draw a line

    Tap the gap between two neighbouring dots to connect them.

  2. Close a box

    Add the fourth side of a square and it becomes yours.

  3. Go again

    Every box you claim earns another turn – chain them for combos.

  4. Win the grid

    When every box is taken, the player with the most wins.
