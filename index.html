Delete your Boxstead account

Applies to accounts created inside the Boxstead app by Cortexlab Studios.

You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time. There's no in-app delete button yet, so you request it by email.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address you signed up with, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  2. Use the subject Boxstead account deletion and include your username.
  3. We'll delete your account and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Or write to hello@ecomfixify.com yourself.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address
  • Your username and profile
  • Your sign-in credentials

What isn't covered

  • Data on your phone, such as achievements and your chosen theme. Uninstall the app or clear its data to remove it.
  • Online match records. These aren't linked to your account, and old ones are deleted on a regular basis.
  • Data held by ad networks. Unity and Meta handle that under their own policies. You can reset your Advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

If you never created an account, we hold no account data about you, and there's nothing to delete.

For the full picture of what Boxstead collects, read the privacy policy.
