Privacy Policy

Boxstead (package com.cortexlabstudios.boxstead) · Cortexlab Studios · Effective October 10, 2026

The short version. You can play Boxstead offline with no account. If you create an account or play online, we store your email, username and match data to make that work. The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network, which may use your advertising ID. We don't sell your personal data. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

This policy explains what information Boxstead ("the game", "we") handles when you use it. Boxstead is published by Cortexlab Studios.

1. Data on your device

Settings (sound, music, haptics), your match history, statistics, unlocked achievements and board themes, and review-prompt timing are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless you choose to share something. Uninstalling the game removes it.

2. Online matches

When you use online matchmaking, the game sends your user ID and display name, your queue status and your moves to our backend so you and your opponent can see the same game in real time. Match and queue records are temporary and are cleared periodically.

3. Accounts

An account is only needed for online play. When you sign up we collect your email address, a username, and a password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we never see it in plain text). We use this only to sign you in, show your name to opponents, and operate the game.

4. Advertising

Boxstead is free and supported by ads. We use Unity LevelPlay to serve ads from Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network. These providers may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interaction data, to deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

  • Unity privacy policy
  • Meta privacy policy

5. Other services

  • Supabase hosts our database and authentication for accounts and online matches.
  • Google Play in-app review may ask you to rate the game; Google handles that interaction under its own policy.
  • Update check: the game may read the latest version number from our backend to tell you when an update is available.

6. Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers above, which process it on our behalf or under their own policies as described, and where required by law. If you share a result image from the game, it goes where you choose to send it.

7. Retention

Account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Temporary match and queue records are removed periodically. Ad-network data is retained by those providers under their own policies.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Play offline without an account.
  • Reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalisation in Android settings.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data. See how to delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. Contact us to exercise them.

9. Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Boxstead is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be noted in the game or on this page.
